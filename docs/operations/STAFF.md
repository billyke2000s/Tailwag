# Staff workflow guide

Use only an individually assigned account with the permissions approved for your duties. Enrol TOTP. Do not share accounts or use screenshots/exports to bypass access restrictions. Your organisation must approve its own care and emergency procedures before live use.

## Daily work

Review handovers, diary/tasks, today's medical work and changes in occupancy. Confirm the animal identifier before entering any record. Medication entries must follow instructions from an authorised professional; record the actual administration, person and time, including missed/refused doses and escalation. Do not invent or infer doses from this software.

At intake record identification/microchip, source/ownership evidence, species, date and known risks. Attach private documents through approved routes. Use hold/quarantine/unavailable flags where applicable; evaluate care and compatible accommodation before placement. Public copy belongs in the explicitly approved website media notes, not internal comments.

## Enquiries and placements

Public adoption/foster/volunteer/contact forms appear in native incoming online forms for manual review. A receipt is not approval. Check existing people/animals before converting enquiries to avoid duplicate records. Complete household assessment and record capacity before foster placement. Check compatibility and actual space. End an existing placement explicitly before creating another; retain returns and reasons. Permanent sanctuary residents remain unavailable for adoption unless an authorised documented decision changes their status.

Review public listings after each availability change. Contact details, foster addresses, ownership documents, sensitive behaviour and medical notes must stay private. Report a mismatched listing or unexpected state immediately.

For an uncertain submission or interrupted change, stop automatic retries and ask an administrator to reconcile the receipt/native records. For incidents, complaints, missing animals, returns, death or end-of-life care, record facts, time, responsible staff and follow-up using the agreed native log/diary/medical/movement records. Escalate urgent welfare concerns through the organisation's real veterinary/emergency route.
