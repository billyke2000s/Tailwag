## Operational translation

Intake must establish identity, source, ownership/transfer authority and care needs before public availability. Care plans include species-appropriate diet, exercise/enrichment, cleaning and professional medical instructions. Quarantine/isolation and compatible accommodation need staff decisions and capacity evidence. Foster/adoption review must assess the household, confirm authority, document the agreement and provide follow-up/return handling. Long-term residents remain in sanctuary care without being marketed for adoption.

Incidents, complaints, missing animals, death and end-of-life decisions need attributed records, escalation and follow-up under approved SOPs. Native logs/diary/medical/movement records support these; local veterinary and safeguarding procedures are still needed. Staff/volunteer duties, training and access must be assigned explicitly. Donations and electronic signatures are optional and require their own approved operational/provider process.

The specification maps these findings to acceptance criteria. Clinical protocols, council decisions, retention durations and actual capacities remain configurable and unverified. No automatic treatment or medication-dose rules are introduced.
